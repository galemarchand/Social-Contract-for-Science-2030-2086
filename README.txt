Supplementary code and data for "Planetary Commons or Fractured Sovereignties? Scenarios for a New Social Contract for Science (2030–2086) after the Artificial Intelligence Shock".

code/figure1.py  - schematic long-wave figure (Figure 1)
code/figure2.py  - historical series and scenario projections (Figure 2)
code/figure3.py  - GERD (% of GDP) by UIS region, observed 2000-2023 and projections to 2086 (Figure 3)
data/            - input data retrieved 23-24 October 2026 (WIPO, SCImago/Scopus, UIS)

Researcher series (Figure 2b) and GDP growth assumptions are embedded in the scripts with their sources.
Scripts require Python 3 with numpy, scipy, matplotlib and openpyxl; file paths at the top of each
script must be adapted to the local folder. All projections are illustrative author assumptions.
